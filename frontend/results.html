Impact Analysis

Group 2 uses a 1024-bit prime modulus. State-level threat actors with Number Field Sieve (NFS) precomputation capabilities can compute discrete logarithms in real time to break master secrets (Logjam variant).

Remediation Directive

Enforce DH Group 14 (2048-bit MODP) as minimum floor, or modernize to Elliptic Curves: Group 19 (256-bit ECP) or Group 21 (Curve25519).
